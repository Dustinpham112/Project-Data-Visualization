First Nations share of hospitalisations, by half-year

How to read this chart: each bar is the share of that half-year's hospitalisations that involved First Nations people. The lighter bar is Jul-Dec 2014: the data publisher hides a few small numbers (shown as “n.p.”) to protect privacy, so that half-year is slightly understated.

Half-year share Jul-Dec 2014 (some values suppressed, so slightly understated)

Shares describe how many hospitalisations involved First Nations people. They are not risk rates, because population size is not taken into account.

About the data

Source: Road Safety Data Hub (BITRE) with AIHW National Hospital Morbidity Database data, 2011-2021 (National, State and territory, First Nations files). Educational use with attribution.

Limits: only hospital admissions are counted; Victoria (2012) and NSW (2017) have series breaks; small numbers are withheld as “n.p.”; figures are counts, not rates.

Accessibility: colour-blind-safe palette, keyboard access to bars and markers, tooltips on focus.
